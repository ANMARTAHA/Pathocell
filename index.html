Ageing: The Telomere Hypothesis

Each replication shortens the telomere a little more, until the cell can no longer safely divide.
NormalShortenedCritical
Telomeres shorten with every division because the ends are not fully replaced. Eventually, division stops or the cell dies — one proposed driver of ageing, alongside oxidative stress, hormonal decline, defective host defense, and failure to renew old cells.
